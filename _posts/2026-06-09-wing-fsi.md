## Project Overview
This project evaluates the aero-elastic behavior of a high-downforce cantilevered racing wing. By implementing a **one-way Fluid-Structure Interaction (FSI)** workflow, transient aerodynamic loads were extracted to determine if the structural deformation stays within strict regulatory limits under peak aerodynamic velocity.

---

## Technical Workflow

### 1. Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD)
* **Software:** Ansys Fluent
* **Setup:** Enclosed computational domain with a velocity inlet matching peak track speeds. A high-resolution inflation layer mesh was generated around the wing profile to resolve the boundary layer ($y^+ \approx 1$).
* **Results:** Extracted the transient pressure distribution across the suction and pressure surfaces of the wing, identifying high-pressure stagnation points and the low-pressure core beneath the main plane.

### 2. Pressure Mapping & Boundary Conditions
* **The Interface:** The surface pressure data from the Fluent solver was exported and mapped onto the structural mesh face inside Ansys Mechanical.
* **Validation:** Conducted a data-mapping validation check to ensure $100\%$ force transfer accuracy across the fluid-solid interface mesh without interpolation losses.

### 3. Finite Element Analysis (FEA)
* **Software:** Ansys Mechanical
* **Materials:** Carbon Fiber Reinforced Polymer (CFRP) layup profile vs. Aluminum 6061-T6 internal ribs.
* **Constraints:** Fixed support at the cantilever mounting chassis plates. 
* **Solver:** Static structural run utilizing the mapped pressure fields as a variable load profile.

---

## Key Engineering Insights & Optimization
* **Mesh Convergence:** Verified consistency by matching the aerodynamic lift/drag coefficients and structural displacement across three distinct mesh densities.
* **Deflection Validation:** The peak tip deflection was evaluated against standard FIA structural regulations (e.g., maximum allowable deflection under a $1000\text{ N}$ point load or equivalent aerodynamic pressure).
* **Mass Optimization:** Identified low-stress zones near the trailing edge framework, paving the way for future weight reduction using periodic lattice structures or localized topology optimization.
