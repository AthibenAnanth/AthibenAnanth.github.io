## Project Overview
This project evaluates the aero-elastic behavior of a high-downforce cantilevered racing wing profile. By implementing a **one-way Fluid-Structure Interaction (FSI)** workflow, transient aerodynamic loads were extracted to determine if the structural deformation stays within strict regulatory limits under peak aerodynamic velocity.

## Technical Workflow & Data Mapping

To achieve an accurate transfer of fluid forces to our structural model, the systems were linked using a unified project schematic. This ensures accurate global force interpolation across disparate fluid and solid mesh configurations.

  <img src="/assets/images/process_diagram.png" alt="Ansys FSI Workflow Schematic" width="70%" />
  <br><em>Figure 2: Linked FSI Project Schematic in Ansys Workbench.</em>

### 1. Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD)
* **Software:** Ansys Fluent
* **Setup:** Enclosed computational domain with a velocity inlet matching peak track speeds. A high-resolution inflation layer mesh was generated around the wing profile to resolve the boundary layer ($y^+ \approx 1$).
* **Results:** Extracted the transient pressure distribution across the suction and pressure surfaces of the wing, identifying high-pressure stagnation points.

<p align="center">
  <img src="/assets/images/pressure_plot.png" alt="Fluent Surface Pressure Contours" width="85%" />
  <br><em>Figure 3: Surface pressure distribution mapping stagnation points on the pressure side.</em>
</p>

### 2. FEA Structural Setup & Boundary Conditions
* **Software:** Ansys Mechanical
* **Materials:** Structural optimization layout utilizing an aluminum internal framework.
* **Constraints:** Fixed support constraints applied directly at the cantilever mounting chassis plates.
* **Loads:** Surface pressure data from the Fluent solver exported and mapped onto the structural mesh face with a $100\%$ force transfer validation check.
